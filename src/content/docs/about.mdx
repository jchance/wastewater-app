## Free for anyone to use

The Field Guide is free for operators, students, trainers, utilities, and anyone else who finds it useful. There are no accounts and no ads.

You can:

- Use it on the job, including at investor-owned and for-profit utilities.
- Teach with it, including in certification training programs that charge tuition.
- Print pages for your plant or classroom.
- Copy, adapt, or host your own version, as long as you credit the Field Guide and share your changes under the same terms.

You can't sell it, put it behind a paywall, or bundle it into a paid product or app. The formulas and facts themselves aren't claimed by anyone, so use them however you like.
